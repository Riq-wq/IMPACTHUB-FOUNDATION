## ⚠️ Why Gmail Marks It as Spam

**Common reasons:**
1. **New sender** - Gmail doesn't recognize noreply@formspree.io
2. **Automated emails** - Gmail's algorithm marks automated emails as spam
3. **No previous interaction** - You never emailed this address before
4. **Common with form services** - Happens with all form services (Formspree, Netlify Forms, etc.)

**This is normal and easy to fix!**
