## 📊 Understanding Formspree's Two Filters

### Filter 1: Formspree's Spam Filter
- **What:** Formspree decides if submission is spam
- **If spam:** Message goes to Formspree spam tab
- **Result:** NO email sent to you
- **Your control:** Can turn OFF in settings

### Filter 2: Gmail's Spam Filter  
- **What:** Gmail decides if email is spam
- **If spam:** Email goes to Gmail spam folder
- **Result:** Email WAS sent, but in spam
- **Your control:** Create Gmail filter

### You Need to Fix BOTH!

## ⚠️ Why Formspree Marked as Spam

Common reasons:
1. **Test submissions** - Formspree thinks they're fake
2. **Empty/short messages** - Looks like spam
3. **Same IP submitting multiple times** - You testing
4. **Generic content** - "Test", "Hi", etc.
5. **New form** - Formspree learning your pattern

**This is normal for new forms with test submissions!**
