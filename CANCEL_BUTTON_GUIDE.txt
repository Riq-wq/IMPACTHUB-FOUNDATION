    M-PESA CANCEL ISSUE - FIXED!
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM:
When you cancel M-Pesa on your phone, the website keeps
loading because M-Pesa doesn't tell us immediately.

SOLUTION:
I've made 3 improvements:

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ FIX #1: REDUCED TIMEOUT

Before: 60 seconds
Now: 30 seconds

Website stops waiting after 30 seconds instead of 60.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ FIX #2: BIG RED CANCEL BUTTON

New button says: "I Cancelled / Stop Waiting"

When you cancel on your phone:
1. Click this red button on website
2. It stops immediately
3. Shows error message
4. You can try again

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ FIX #3: HELPFUL MESSAGE

Timer now shows:
"Cancelled on your phone? Click the button below to stop
waiting."

This reminds users to click the cancel button.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HOW TO USE:

1. Make donation
2. M-Pesa prompt appears on phone
3. If you cancel on phone:
   → Click "I Cancelled / Stop Waiting" button on website
   → It stops immediately ✅

4. If you ignore it:
   → Waits 30 seconds
   → Then shows error automatically

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY THIS HAPPENS:

M-Pesa doesn't tell us when you cancel immediately.
It keeps saying "pending" even after you cancelled.

So we can't detect it automatically - you need to tell
us by clicking the cancel button!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TIMELINE:

Old Way:
- Cancel on phone → Wait 60 seconds → Error ❌

New Way:
- Cancel on phone → Click button → Instant error ✅
