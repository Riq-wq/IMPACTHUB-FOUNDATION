## ⚠️ TROUBLESHOOTING: If You Don't See Messages

### **Issue 1: Emails Going to Spam**

**Why it happens:**
- Formspree emails sometimes marked as spam
- Gmail's automatic filtering

**Solution:**
1. Check Spam/Junk folder
2. If you find Formspree emails there:
   - Mark as "Not Spam"
   - Add `noreply@formspree.io` to contacts
3. Future emails will go to inbox

---

### **Issue 2: Formspree Not Verified**

**Why it happens:**
- When you first set up Formspree
- You need to verify your email

**Solution:**
1. Check your email for Formspree verification email
2. Click the verification link
3. After verification, all messages will be delivered

**Verification Email Subject:**
- "Activate your form on Formspree"
